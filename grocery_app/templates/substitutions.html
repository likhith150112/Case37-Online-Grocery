How FreshCart Substitution Logic Operates
1. Category Matching: Replacements must belong to the identical category (e.g., Dairy with Dairy, Staples with Staples).
2. Price Ceiling Rule: Suggested replacement cannot exceed the original item price by more than 20% without customer consent.
3. Dietary & Allergen Safety: Items with customer-flagged allergens (gluten, tree-nuts, lactose) are strictly eliminated from suggestions.
